Character design sheet for a Korean educational game set in 1565, Joseon dynasty, at Dosan Seodang (a small Confucian study hall). Four characters stand side by side, full body, facing the viewer, evenly spaced, same scale, on a plain warm paper background. From left to right:
1) The elderly Confucian scholar-teacher, about 65 years old, slender and upright, calm gentle face with kind eyes, neat thin white beard, wearing a plain white scholar's robe (simui) with black trim at the collar and sleeves, a black soft scholar's cap, holding a thread-bound book. Humble and warm, not grand.
2) A Korean boy of about ten, round cheerful face, bright curious eyes, hair in one long single braid down his back tied with a red ribbon, pale jade-green jeogori jacket, white baji trousers tied at the ankles, straw sandals, carrying a small cloth book bundle.
3) A middle-aged village herbal doctor, black horsehair gat hat, grey-blue robe, a wooden medicine box on his back, holding a small bowl of dark herbal medicine.
4) A middle-aged villager in plain hemp clothes with a white towel tied around his head, a gossiping expression, one hand raised as if telling a rumor.
Each character must be clearly distinguishable by silhouette and main color.
Art style: Korean true-view landscape ink painting (jingyeong sansu) with light watercolor tints (sumuk damchae), in the manner of 18th-century Korean literati painters: expressive calligraphic brush strokes, dry-brush texture strokes on rocks, dotted moss on pines, soft transparent washes of pale indigo, light ochre and faint green, generous white space, visible warm hanji paper texture, elegant and restrained. Absolutely no text, no letters, no calligraphy, no seals, no signatures, no captions.
