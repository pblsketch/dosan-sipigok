A tall vertical panel of a Korean folding screen (byeongpung) painting. Scene: Dosan Seodang, a tiny, humble Joseon-dynasty Confucian study hall of the 1560s (a three-bay wooden house with a grey tiled roof, a small wooden veranda, a brushwood fence and a brushwood gate) on a gentle hillside among pine trees, seen from slightly above. Below it a wide calm river bends around a rocky cliff, with a deep pool and a flat rock at the water's edge. Layered mountains fade into mist in the distance; soft spring evening. On the veranda sits a small elderly Korean scholar in a plain white robe with a black scholar's cap, and on the path below a small Korean boy of about ten in a pale jade-green jeogori and white baji, hair in one long single braid tied with a red ribbon, carrying a book bundle. The people are small in the landscape. Composition leaves generous empty space (sky, mist, water) at the top and bottom for text overlay. Absolutely no text, no letters, no calligraphy, no seals, no signatures, no captions.

Art style: modern painterly illustration for a quiet indie mobile game, inspired by East Asian ink wash: soft layered washes, simplified shapes, gentle gradients of mist, a restrained palette of ink grey, pale jade, soft peach and warm paper white, subtle paper grain, clean and highly readable at small sizes, calm and luminous like the games Gris or Alba.
